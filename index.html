Working together

There are three ways we can work together.

01

Projects

Websites, apps and landing pages, from the first brief through to launch. I start with the people who will use it and how the content is organised, before any visual design.

What it usually includes
  • Requirements, audience and a sitemap
  • Wireframes and a clickable prototype
  • Visual design for desktop and mobile
  • Developer handover and a check of the build before launch

How it starts A free call to talk through what you need, then a fixed quote.

02

Monthly design

For small teams who need a designer most weeks, but not forty hours of one. You get a set number of hours each month for whatever comes up, from someone who already knows your brand.

Good for
  • Landing pages and website updates
  • Email newsletters, social posts and ads
  • Brochures, flyers and other print work
  • Small changes to an existing app or portal

How it starts A free call to agree the hours and how requests come through.

03

Small jobs

One landing page, an ad set, a brochure, a book cover or a pack design, with a clear brief and a quick turnaround.

How it works
  • Send the brief and any brand files you have
  • Agree a fixed price before any work starts
  • Receive files ready for print or the web

How it starts Email the brief, or book a free call if it needs talking through.
